# Romanian Monetary Dynamics v0.1.0

**Scientific-core baseline — initial research release**

RMD v0.1.0 is the first formal public snapshot after consolidation around the scientific model core.

## Included

- accounting spine and structural dynamics contracts;
- empirical-dynamics mechanism and evidence registries;
- source/processed data and provenance;
- validation-recovery selection, holdout and disposition artifacts;
- government repricing ledger assessment;
- Python scientific/reference implementation and reproducibility scripts.

The previous end-user/product layer is intentionally not part of this release.

## Scientific status at the v0.1.0 snapshot

At the v0.1.0 snapshot, the scientific stage was **Validation Recovery / Empirical Basis Expansion** and the canonical disposition recorded **0 validated reference behavioural mechanisms**.

The household housing-lending pass-through form remained a candidate because its fresh final holdout contained no policy-rate movement and therefore could not discriminate the candidate from persistence. NFC pass-through candidates failed the structural-selection improvement gate before final holdout evaluation. The government refinancing/effective-rate mechanism remained deferred because the required matched instrument-level structure was not identifiable without prohibited synthetic allocation.

The accounting spine remained a hard constraint, and behavioural closure was not active in the canonical dynamic core.

This file describes the historical v0.1.0 release snapshot. `STATUS.md` is the maintained source for the repository's current scientific boundary.
