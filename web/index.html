ACCOUNTING / EXPOSURE STRESS TEST

Mechanical exposure tests — not macroeconomic forecasts

These tests change an accounting price, valuation or stock input and show only direct balance-sheet exposure. They do not simulate consumption, investment, credit supply, exchange-rate reactions, fiscal reaction or unvalidated behavioural feedbacks.
